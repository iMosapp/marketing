i'M On Social: Manager Playbook

The whole job for a manager fits on a few pages. Onboard a rep in ten minutes, give them a number, set up the store once, and look at five screens every Monday. Everything below matches the app exactly as it looks today (June 2026).

App Store link for reps: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/im-on-social/id6774618559

Web version: https://app.imonsocial.com

1. How the app is laid out

Five tabs along the bottom: Home, Contacts, Inbox, Activity, Tools.

Salespeople see a focused app. Home is three cards and one gold + button. Tools has five folders. Nothing else. A rep who clears the three cards and the Inbox every morning is doing the job.

Managers, admins and partners see everything. Extra folders appear in Tools (Leads, Manage, Campaigns, My Performance, Set Up) and extra cards on Home (Leads Waiting, AI Reply Health, Hot This Week, Weekly Wins, Book of Business).

Want to see what a rep sees? Tools > View as Rep (small link under your name).

Rep Home, top to bottom

  • Do This Next: one action already chosen for them. Gold button to do it, Skip if not today.
  • Your 3 for Today: three people worth a 30-second text, with the reason. Each card: Text (message already written), Check (handled it another way), X (skip).
  • Needs a Reply: conversations waiting on a human. Red WAITING badge = Jessi handed off.
  • Gold + button: SOLD!, Calendar, Send Photo, Voice Note, Make a Call.
  • Header: QR icon (their card), AI ON / AI PAUSED, bell (Alerts).

2. Onboard a rep in 10 minutes

Invite (2 minutes)

Tools > Set Up > Invite Team. First name, last name, email, mobile number (this is where the activation code goes), role Salesperson, tap Send.

The rep immediately gets a text and an email with the App Store link and three steps. A Copy button appears for you so you can paste the same message into your own thread with them.

What the rep does (3 minutes)

  1. Install i'M On Social from the App Store.
  2. Open it, tap Activate my account under the login button.
  3. Enter their mobile number, enter the 6-digit code, choose a password.
  4. Tap Allow when iPhone asks about notifications.

They land on Home.

Give them a number (2 minutes)

Tools > Set Up > Phone Numbers > Buy. See section 3. A rep without a number cannot text customers and Jessi cannot answer for them.

Profile and training (their first 15 minutes)

  • Tools > Profile: headshot, title, two-sentence bio. This powers their Digital Card, Link Page, Landing Page and Showcase.
  • Tools > Learning > Training Hub > Sales Team Onboarding: 8 lessons, about 30 minutes.
  • Tools > Settings > My VA: how they talk, what they know. Tap Hear Your VA in Action.

3. Phone numbers: one screen

Tools > Set Up > Phone Numbers. Everything about numbers happens here.

Buy and assign in one step

  1. Tap Buy.
  2. Under Assign to, tap the rep (or leave Number pool).
  3. Enter the local area code, tap Search.
  4. Tap Buy & assign on any number.

Texting, calling, voicemail and Jessi are wired automatically. Numbers cost $1.15 a month each. Local area codes get more replies.

Reading the list

  • ASSIGNED + rep name: their customers text this number, Jessi answers as them.
  • POOL: no rep. Jessi answers as the store and hands leads to whoever is available.
  • Texts and calls OK: healthy. Needs a fix: tap Fix, it repairs the connection in a second.
  • Contacts, messages this month and last activity tell you if a number is actually being used.

Reassign / Move to Pool: tap Reassign on any number. The number keeps working the whole time; customers never notice.

Release: gives the number back to the carrier and stops billing. Cannot be undone. Prefer Move to Pool.

4. Set up the store for Jessi (once)

Jessi is the AI assistant on every number. She answers inbound texts within seconds, in the rep's voice, 24/7, and hands off with a You're Needed alert when a human is needed (pricing, payments, frustration, "can I talk to someone"). She is only as good as what the store gives her.

5. Your weekly rhythm (Monday, ten minutes)

  1. Home > Leads Waiting: anything unanswered from the weekend gets claimed now.
  2. Home > AI Reply Health: if Jessi failed to send anything, it shows here with the reason.
  3. Tools > My Performance > Leaderboard: touches, replies, reviews, sales by rep. Praise the top, ask the bottom what got in the way.
  4. Tools > My Performance > Team Tasks: overdue follow-ups by rep. Overdue means a customer is waiting.
  5. Home > Hot This Week: the vehicles shoppers opened most. Tell the floor.

The three numbers that matter

  • Reply time on WAITING threads. Under five minutes during shift.
  • Touches per rep per day. Five is the floor; Your 3 for Today gets them to three before coffee.
  • Review conversion: reviews earned divided by review requests sent.

Coaching with receipts: open any rep's contact from Team Tasks or the Activity tab and read the thread. Coach on what was actually said.

6. Giving a rep more (or less)

Tools > Set Up > Team Members > open the rep > Permissions. Turn on Campaigns, Broadcast, Internet Leads or reports for that person. They appear in their Tools the next time they open the app.

Default is deliberately small. Reporting is your screen, not theirs.

7. When a rep leaves

The relationships belong to the store.

  1. Tools > Set Up > Phone Numbers: Reassign their number to the new rep, or Move to Pool. Customers who text the old number reach the store, not a dead line.
  2. Move their contacts: iMOS admins do this in Tools > Admin > Bulk Transfer (to one rep, or split across the team). Notes, dates, voice-note memories and history come along. If you do not see Bulk Transfer, ask your iMOS admin.
  3. Tools > Set Up > Team Members: open the rep and Deactivate. Login stops immediately; their number is held in the pool.

The new rep's Your 3 for Today starts surfacing those customers the next morning, with the reasons, as if they had known them all along.

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Rep cannot text customersNo number, or it is in the pool. Tools > Set Up > Phone Numbers > assign one. If it says Needs a fix, tap Fix.
Jessi is not answeringCheck AI ON on the rep's Home (it may say AI PAUSED). Open the thread: if the rep tapped Take Over, Jessi stays quiet until Resume AI. Failed sends: Home > AI Reply Health.
No push notificationsTools > Settings > Notifications > Push Health Check > Send me a test push. The card says what is off; usually iPhone Settings > Notifications > i'M On Social is turned off, or quiet hours are on. Every push is also mirrored in Alerts (bell).
Inventory missing a vehicleTools > Manage > Inventory Feed > Pull now. Still missing = not in the vendor's feed.
Lead came in, nobody was calledTools > Leads > Lead Source Config > that source: check the call ladder is on and the texting window / store hours (after-hours leads ring at opening). Team Availability: is anyone on shift?
Rep says "I don't know what to do"Home. Three cards. Do them. Then Tools > Learning > Training Hub.

9. What to tell a new rep (say this out loud)

"Open the app every morning. Home tells you exactly what to do: one Do This Next, three people to text, and anyone waiting on a reply. Tap Text, the message is already written, fix it if you want, send. When you sell a car, hit the gold plus and SOLD!. After the handshake, hit Voice Note and tell the app what you learned about them. That's it. Jessi handles the texts you miss. When she needs you, your phone buzzes."
