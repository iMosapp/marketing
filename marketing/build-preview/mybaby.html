What it actually does

A CRM stores what happened. i'M On Social understands what changed.

Something happens. The relationship changes. The system understands the change, and the next action changes with it. Every conversation, call and customer signal strengthens the record, so the platform can determine:

Who needs attention→Why→What should happen next→When→How to execute it

Every interaction makes the next interaction smarter.

One platform. One shared context.

Six systems most companies buy from six vendors. One relationship layer connecting all of them.

The texting system knows what the relationship engine knows. So does voice. So does automation, training and tasks. Not six features under one login: six systems sharing one memory.

Persistent customer memory that turns conversations, activities, preferences, milestones and engagement history into a continuously evolving relationship profile.

AI communication

Automation engine

Sales execution

Lead response, customer engagement, landing pages, digital experiences, reviews, referrals, communication tools and actionable next steps.

Training & coaching

AI practice calls, role-play scenarios, scoring, scripts, coaching, progress tracking and certification.

Enterprise infrastructure

Multi-user organizations, permissions, multi-location deployments, billing, partner programs, integrations, reporting and APIs.
