Built for turnover

Salespeople leave.
Their customers shouldn't leave with them.

Every business knows the drill: a rep quits and 500 relationships walk out the door on a personal cell phone. Customers keep texting the old number, nobody answers, and the company starts over from zero.

With i'M On Social, the company owns the phone number and every conversation attached to it. When a rep leaves, the number, the texts, the notes and the birthdays all stay with the company. The next rep picks up mid-conversation, and the customer never even feels the handoff.

  • Company-owned tracking numbers. Reps text and call from a number you control, not their personal cell.
  • Relationships stay in your CRM. Contacts, message history, voice notes and follow-ups live in the company's book of business.
  • One-tap reassignment. Move a departing rep's people to a new rep with the full history intact, then keep the number ringing.
  • Nothing to chase down. No begging for a personal phone, no lost spreadsheets, no "who was working with Mike?"
