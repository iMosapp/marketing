Questions

Pricing questions

How does billing work?

Plans are billed monthly by card. Individual is $100 per month. Teams are $75 per user per month with a 5-seat minimum, so $375 per month to start.

Can we add people later?

Yes. Each additional person on a team plan is $75 per month.

Is there a free trial?

We start every account with a demo so we can set it up around your business. There is no self-serve sign-up yet.

Do we need to replace our CRM?

No. i'M On Social works next to the CRM you already use. Leads can come in by email, ADF or webhook.

Does it work on Android?

The app is on iPhone and the web today. Android users can use the web app in their browser.
