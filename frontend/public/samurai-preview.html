Decision Guide
Reasons to do it
• SamurAI is a stronger, more memorable brand
• Red/black/white = high contrast, premium, aggressive — fits the sales closer persona
• "Remember Everyone. Close More. Forever." is a killer tagline
• Easier to trademark "SamurAI" than "i'M On Social"
• Better App Store name for discoverability
Things to consider
• New domain needed (samurai.ai or samurai.vi or similar)
• Existing users bookmarked app.imonsocial.com — need redirect
• 10DLC brand registration would need to be updated
• Any printed materials / business cards / pitch decks
• "by VI" — does that stay as a subtitle or disappear in-app?
Total scope: 3–5 working days for a complete rebrand across the entire codebase, marketing site, and all presentations. The color system change (1 line in themeStore.ts) cascades to 100 files but is scriptable. Say "apply SamurAI rebrand" when you're ready.
